A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you join.

The first cohort is intentionally small. Joining the list does not create an account or guarantee a beta spot—it gives us a way to invite the right mix of working artists when testing begins.

Who is Star Artist being built for?

The first testing focus is freelance makeup artists and hairstylists. Star Artist is ultimately designed around artists whose work revolves around clients, sessions, collaboration, travel, and running a creative business.

Is Star Artist available now?

Not publicly. Star Artist is currently preparing for a limited Founding Beta so we can test it in real working situations before a broader release.

What happens when I sign up?

Your email is added to the early-access list. When beta invitations begin, selected artists will receive details about access, testing, and what participation looks like.

Does signing up create a Star Artist account?

No. This page is only for early access and beta interest. A Star Artist account is created later if you are invited into testing.

What will beta testers actually do?

Use Star Artist in real work, tell us where it helps, where it slows you down, what feels missing, and what should be improved before launch.
