Privacy

Star Artist collects the information you provide when you request early access—such as your email address and any optional details you choose to share about your business.

We use this information to send you updates about the Star Artist beta, to select and invite testers, and to understand the needs of freelance makeup artists and hairstylists. We do not create a Star Artist account for you when you sign up.

You can unsubscribe from product updates at any time using the link in any update email. Your signup details are kept only as long as needed for the beta program.

For questions about your data, contact the Star Artist team.
